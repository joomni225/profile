## Platform features

### Website builder and design

Start from a theme, then shape every page visually. What you see in the editor is what visitors get, on phones, tablets and desktops.

- **Theme store:** ready-made designs for shops, salons, travel, restaurants and more. Preview live and install in one click.
- **Visual page builder:** build pages from sections such as heroes, product grids, testimonials, galleries and forms. Drag to reorder, edit in place and undo anything.
- **Custom code sections:** add your own HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a full-screen code editor.
- **Liquid templates:** loops, conditions and filters that read your live catalog, so one template renders every product page.
- **Menus and navigation:** drag-and-drop header and footer menus with dropdowns, linking to pages, products, collections or any web address.
- **Blog:** publish guides, news and stories that bring in visitors from Google.
- **Media library:** upload once, organize in folders and reuse anywhere.
- **Your own domain with free SSL:** start on `yourstore.joomni.com`, then connect `yourbrand.com` or `yourbrand.com.np`.
- **Redirects:** keep your Google rankings when you rename pages or move from another platform.

### Online store and e-commerce

A complete store: catalog, checkout, customer accounts and the order tools to handle everything after someone buys.

- **Products and variants:** sizes, colors and options, each with its own price and stock, plus photos, specs, barcodes and search settings.
- **Collections:** group products by hand, or set rules so matching products join automatically.
- **Brands:** record the brands you stock and show their logos on your site.
- **Checkout and customer accounts:** guest checkout, optional or required accounts, and sign-in with email or Google.
- **Order management:** edit orders after they arrive, create orders by hand for phone and social sales, print invoices and keep a full order timeline.
- **Returns and refunds:** handle returns and exchanges, refund to the original payment or as store credit, and restock returned items.
- **Cash on delivery protection:** flag new COD orders for a confirmation call and block repeat no-show numbers.
- **Discounts and offers:** codes, automatic festival sales, free shipping over a minimum spend and buy-X-get-Y deals.

### Local payments and delivery

- **eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay and Fonepay QR:** connect your own merchant accounts. Joomni never touches your money.
- **Payment QR and cash on delivery.**
- **Cards, bank transfers and Stripe** for international sales.
- **Shipping zones and rates:** set where you deliver, what it costs and free delivery over a minimum spend.
- **Pathao and Upaya pickups:** book a courier pickup straight from an order and track it, without retyping addresses.

- **Works offline:** keep selling when the internet drops. Sales sync when you are back online.
- **Real-time inventory sync** between your shop counter and your online store.
- **Barcode scanning and receipt printing** over USB or Bluetooth.
- **Split payments:** cash, card, eSewa, Khalti and Fonepay QR in one sale.
- **Staff accounts:** track sales by employee.
- **Unified sales reports** for online and in-store sales.
- **Quick checkout grid** for fast counters.

## Manage your store with Claude or ChatGPT (MCP)

Joomni supports **MCP (Model Context Protocol)**, the open standard that lets AI apps connect to the tools you use. Connect Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP-compatible app to your store and manage it by chatting, using your own AI subscription.

### What you can ask

- **Sales and orders:** "How did we do last week compared with the week before?"
- **Stock and products:** "What is about to run out?"
- **Customers:** "Who are my best customers this month?"
- **Building pages:** "Add a pricing page with three tiers."
- **SEO:** "Check every page for missing titles and descriptions and write better ones."
- **Bookings:** "What is in the diary for Saturday?"

### You stay in control

- **Read-only by default.** A new connection can answer questions and change nothing.
- **You approve every change.** Changes are described first and wait for your yes.
- **Separate permission for custom code,** which stays hidden on a live page until you unhide it.
- **One store only.** The connection can only reach the store you choose.
- **Full activity log** and one-click disconnect.
- **No extra AI cost.** Your Claude or ChatGPT subscription does the thinking.

AI connections are included on the **Business and Enterprise** plans. [Learn more](https://joomni.com/features/ai-connections)

### How to connect Joomni to Claude

1. Sign in or sign up at [joomni.com](https://joomni.com)
2. Open your dashboard, then go to **Settings**
3. Open the **MCP Connection** tab
4. Copy your **MCP URL**
5. Open Claude, go to connector settings and add a new connector
6. Paste your URL, sign in to Joomni and allow access
7. Start a new chat, turn on Joomni and just ask

> Keep your MCP URL private. Treat it like a key to your store.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Joomni?**
Joomni is a no-code website builder, online store and POS platform made for businesses in Nepal.

**Do I need technical knowledge?**
No. Joomni is made for business owners and creatives. Themes and the visual page builder cover almost everything, and custom code is optional.

**Which payment methods does Joomni support in Nepal?**
eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay and Fonepay QR, plus payment QR codes and cash on delivery. You connect your own merchant accounts, and Joomni takes no commission on your sales.

**Can I use my own domain?**
Yes. Every store starts on a free `yourstore.joomni.com` address, and you can connect your own `.com` or `.com.np` domain with free SSL.

**Can Joomni handle delivery?**
Yes. Set shipping zones and rates, and book Pathao and Upaya pickups straight from an order on the Business plan.

**Does the POS work without internet?**
Yes. It keeps selling offline and syncs when the connection returns.

**Can I take appointments and bookings?**
Yes. Customers book online and you manage everything from one calendar.

**Does Joomni help with SEO?**
Yes. You get editable meta titles, descriptions and slugs, an automatic sitemap, schema markup and redirects.

**Can I manage my store from Claude or ChatGPT?**
Yes. Connect any MCP-compatible app to ask about orders and stock or build pages by chatting. It is read-only by default and you approve every change. Included on Business and Enterprise plans.

**How is Joomni different from Shopify or Wix?**
Joomni combines a drag-and-drop website builder with a full e-commerce engine, with native Nepal payments and delivery built in.

**How do I try Joomni?**
Create a free account. You get a 14-day free trial of the Business plan, with no card needed.
